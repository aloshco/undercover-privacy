Undercover — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 April 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Undercover mobile app ("the App", "we") handles information when you use it.

What We Collect Directly

The App is an offline party word game. We do not require an account and we do not operate any servers that receive your data. All gameplay data (player names, selected settings, game history) is stored only on your device using iOS's standard local storage (UserDefaults) and never leaves your device through us.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To deliver ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) — only if you grant the App tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • Coarse location (derived from IP address)
  • Device information (device model, OS version, language)
  • Information about your interactions with ads (impressions, clicks)

If you allow tracking when prompted, AdMob may use this information to serve you personalized ads and to measure ad performance across apps and websites.

If you do not allow tracking, AdMob serves only non-personalized ads. Limited information (such as approximate location and basic device info) is still used to fulfill ad requests, but it is not used to build a profile of you.

You can change your tracking choice anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

For more on Google's privacy practices, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Data Stored on Your Device

The following items are saved locally on your device only:

  • Chosen app language and game word language
  • Player name history (so previous names can be re-used)
  • Player count, undercover count, and discussion time preferences

You can remove all of this data at any time by deleting the App from your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any user.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, there is nothing for us to access, modify, or delete on your behalf. For data collected by Google AdMob, see Google's privacy controls linked above.

Changes to This Policy
